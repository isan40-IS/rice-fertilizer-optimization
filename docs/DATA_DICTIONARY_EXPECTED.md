# Expected Source Columns

## Primary

- `crop`
- `yield.wb.kg_ha`
- `N.rate.kg_ha`
- `P.rate.kg_ha`
- `K.rate.kg_ha`
- `Nutrients applied (NPKS)`

## Candidate state/context

- `soil_type`
- `fertilizer_type`
- `avprec.mm`
- `initial.ph.soil`
- `soc.percentage`
- `som.percentage`
- `avtemp.Cdegree`
- `N_source`
- `P_source`
- `K_source`

## Traceability and validation

- `id`
- `exp.id`
- `year`
- `location`

## Excluded by default

- `S.rate.kg_ha`
- `N.nitrate.soil.layer1`
- `elevation.m`
- `slope.perc`
- `yield.db.kg_ha`
- `P.soil.layer1`
- `K.soil.layer1`

These may be reintroduced only after coverage, units, depths, and measurement methods are resolved.
